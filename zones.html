The five zones

The ocean is layered. Each layer is defined by how much light reaches it, and that shapes everything that lives there.

Common questions

Why is the deep sea so cold?

Sunlight only warms the top layer. Cold, dense water from the poles sinks and spreads along the bottom of the ocean.

Why don't deep animals get crushed?

Many have bodies made mostly of water and no air spaces, so the pressure inside matches the pressure outside.

How do we know what lives down there?

Crewed submersibles, remotely operated vehicles, baited cameras and nets all bring back samples and video.
