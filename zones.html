The five zones

The ocean is layered. Each layer is defined by how much sunlight reaches it, and that shapes everything that lives there.

Zones at a glance

Conditions in each ocean zone (approximate values)
Zone Depth (metres) Light Temperature
From To

Twilight 2001,000 A dim blue glow. No photosynthesis. Falls quickly with depth
Midnight 1,0004,000 None. Only living light. About 2 to 4 °C
