Creatures of the deep

Three animals, one from each of the first three zones. Press a button to reveal a fact.

Moon jelly

Sunlight zone

Depth
0 to 50 m
Size
Up to 40 cm wide

The four pale rings you can see through its bell are its reproductive organs.

Giant squid

Twilight zone

Depth
300 to 1,000 m
Size
Up to about 13 m long

It has the largest eyes of any animal, about the size of a dinner plate.

Anglerfish

Midnight zone

Depth
1,000 to 4,000 m
Size
Females up to about 1 m

Its lure glows because glowing bacteria live inside it.

More animals to look up

  • Lanternfish, a small fish covered in light organs
  • Barreleye, a fish with a see-through head
  • Vampire squid, which can flip its arms over its body
  • Yeti crab, found near hot vents on the seafloor
